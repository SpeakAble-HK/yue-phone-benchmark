Scale (standard 5-point ACR anchors): bad 1.0–1.8, poor 1.8–2.6, fair 2.6–3.4, good 3.4–4.2, excellent 4.2–5.0. DNSMOS P.835: SIG = speech, BAK = background, OVRL = overall. SIGMOS P.804: COL/DISC/LOUD/NOISE/REVERB/SIG/OVRL. Per-clip distributions (◆ = mean):

Fine-tuned wav2vec2 with BERT-style Jyutping modeling; ~6k hours Cantonese with iterative pseudo-label correction. Served on GPU via /v1/phoneme (~0.03–0.05 RTF).

5. Confusion analysis (onset / rime / coda / tone)

Syllables split four ways (rime = vocalic nucleus incl. i/u glides; coda ∈ m/n/ng/p/t/k/∅). Component accuracy: onset 0.877, rime 0.870, coda 0.862, tone 0.858.

Top patterns: coda n→∅ (x11), t→k (x9); tone 6→4 / 6→1 (x6 each); onset z→d (x5); rime u→o, yu→a. Weak spots: nasal/stop codas and tone 6.

6. Takeaways

  • yue-phone v2 baseline: 14/50 exact, mean SER 0.232 (median 0.139).
  • Hardest slice: unknown (0.369); easiest: studio_reference (0.093).
  • Error mass sits in codas (−n drop, −t→−k) and tone 6 — acoustic-model work.
