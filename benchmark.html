§2 Audio quality · DNSMOS + SIGMOS

The voices are fine. The background is what makes these clips sound poor.

Overall quality averages poor (both OVRL means are about 2.2–2.3). The low scores come from background noise (BAK and NOISE about 2.5), while the speech itself rates fair to good (SIG about 3.2, DISC about 3.5). Reverb is not a problem (about 3.9). Pick a condition to see where its clips fall.

§3 System under test

Yue Phone v2

Architecture
Fine-tuned wav2vec2-BERT recognizer that outputs toned Jyutping syllables per clip.
Training
About 6k hours of Cantonese speech with iterative pseudo-label correction. These details come from the model provider and were not verified here.
Serving
ONNX Runtime on GPU (RTX 3080), about 0.03–0.05 RTF end to end. Queried on 2026-11-03 via POST /v1/phoneme (multipart file, 16 kHz WAV) at https://yue-phone.hon9kon9ize.com.
Run
results/yue-phone_20261103/hypotheses.{csv,json}. All 50 of 50 clips were transcribed with no failures.
